# 03. Valkompass Experience

## Research

- The current app is primarily a chat UI under `src/features/chat/`.
- The frontend does not yet guide users through issues, priorities, or party comparisons.
- A better valkompass experience needs source-backed party comparisons, not just open-ended chat.

## Plan

1. Add a guided issue profile flow.
2. Let users choose issues, stance, and importance.
3. Generate source-backed party comparisons from the profile.
4. Show matches, differences, and cited explanations.
5. Keep chat available for follow-up questions.
6. Add a source view for inspecting cited documents and URLs.
